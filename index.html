Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes — you can upgrade or downgrade your subscription at any time. When upgrading, the new plan takes effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades apply at the end of your current billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI, net banking, and wallet payments. Enterprise plans also support wire transfer and purchase orders.

Is there a free trial?

Every new account receives a 14-day free trial of the Veritas Pro plan. No credit card required. At the end of the trial you can choose to continue with Pro or switch to the Researcher plan.

How does the Skeptic Agent improve research quality?

The Skeptic Agent acts as an adversarial auditor — it actively attempts to disprove claims, detect p-hacking, identify citation bias, and flag methodological weaknesses. This counter-intelligence process significantly increases the epistemic integrity of every verified report.

What is the Merkle Audit Trail?

Every claim verified by VERITAS is cryptographically sealed using SHA-256 hash trees (Merkle trees). This provides an immutable, tamper-proof record of the entire evidence chain — from raw source to final report — enabling full provenance traceability.
